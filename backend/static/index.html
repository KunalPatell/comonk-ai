STAR Answer Framework

S
Situation
Set the scene in 2–3 sentences. Include team size, timeline, and business context so interviewers understand the scope.
T
Task
Define your specific role and responsibility. What were you personally accountable for? Be precise — avoid "we."
A
Action
Walk through what YOU did, step by step. Focus on decisions, trade-offs, and the technical or interpersonal depth of your approach.
R
Result
Quantify the outcome. Use metrics: revenue impact, latency reduction, user growth, cost saved. If no numbers exist, describe the qualitative change.
